Short bio

Tomas Havranek is Professor of Economics at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, Prague. He studies meta-analysis, correcting published evidence for selective reporting and biases in study design. With Zuzana Irsova, he maintains meta-analysis.cz. He is a CEPR and Stanford METRICS Research Affiliate and Associate Editor for meta-analysis at the Journal of Economic Surveys. Formerly Advisor to the Board of the Czech National Bank, he became the first Czech economist on an ERC Advanced Grant panel in 2020. He lives in Litomysl, chairs the local school board and helps run the town's floorball club.
