Teaching

Besides regular economics teaching, I give specialized courses on meta-analysis: a full university course at the University of Canterbury (2025), two days for policy economists in Stockholm (2025), a three-hour course at the University of Osaka (2026) and a PhD workshop session in Chemnitz (2026). The slides, code, data and, where available, recordings and transcripts are free at meta-analysis.cz/teaching.

Tools

EasyMeta lets you run your own meta-analysis online. To stress-test a paper before referees do, use our MAD app: four AI models read it in different roles and cross-examine each other. For Claude Code, our paper-workshop has referees from rival traditions argue over the paper and then rebuild it. All our tools, including a pre-review for ERC proposals, are on one page.
